About This Project

Information Visualization

Authors: Estêvão Abreu, João Luiz Castanheira & José Pedro Gomes

This project explores reported UFO sightings from 1906 to 2014, aiming to visualize global patterns, trends over time, and correlations between location, shape, and duration of sightings.

This is an academic project, developed for the "Information Visualization" course unit of the Master's in Design and Multimedia of the Faculty of Sciences and Technology of the University of Coimbra, during the school year of 2025/2026.

Original dataset provided by the National UFO Reporting Center (NUFORC) via Kaggle.
